# Solace — Software Developer Co-op, Reliability

**May 2026 – August 2026 · Ottawa, ON**

Most of this Term was Migration Work, which nobody throws a Party for and
everybody needs.

I moved Production Microservices across breaking major Versions — Auditing,
Billing, Authentication, and the SRE Ops Console — onto Spring Boot 4 and React
19, retiring End-of-Life Dependencies in the Process.

The Test Suite came with Me. A legacy Aurelia Cypress E2E Suite had to be
re-instrumented onto a new React Micro-Frontend Host before CI/CD could run
clean again.

Along the Way I repaired broken Account Activation and Navigation Regressions in
the Shell, which is the Part of Onboarding a Customer sees before They decide
how They feel about the Product.

The Piece I liked most: Solace Cloud is resold through SAP and Boomi, and the
Product Naming was hard-coded. I replaced It with Configuration-driven Branding
so White-labelled Deployments could meet Enterprise Partner Standards without a
Fork.
