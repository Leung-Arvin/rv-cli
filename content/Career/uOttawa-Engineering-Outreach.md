# University of Ottawa — Full-Stack Web Developer Co-op, Engineering Outreach

**January 2025 – April 2025 · Ottawa, ON**

My first Co-op, and the first Time I owned something End to End.

through the Angular UI — so Staff could track Emergency Calls instead of
reconstructing Them afterwards from Memory.

The other Half of the Term was a legacy Room-Booking Page that Staff used daily
and nobody enjoyed. I refactored It and leaned on Jasmine to prove I had changed
