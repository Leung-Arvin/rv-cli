# Why a Terminal

Every Portfolio Site looks the same because every Portfolio Site is solving the
same Problem badly: how do I seem impressive to a Stranger in nine Seconds.

The Answer the Industry landed on is a Hero Image, three Cards, and a Sentence
about being passionate. I have read hundreds of Them. I could not tell You a
single Detail from any of Them.

So I built a Thing that requires You to type. It is worse by every Metric a
Marketer would use — higher Bounce Rate, no SEO to speak of, actively hostile on
a Phone. But the People who do type `ls` are, by Definition, curious, and Those
are the only People I want to talk to anyway.

The other Reason is less noble: I wanted to see how far I could get with
xterm.js and an Edge Runtime, and a Portfolio is the only Project where I am
both the Engineer and the entire Product Team.

Run `cat ../Projects/rv-cli.md` if You want the Architecture.
